Term Sherpa

A free browser extension that explains the terms, privacy policy or “I agree” box in front of you, in plain English, before you click.

What it does

  • Click the Term Sherpa button on a page with an “I agree” box, a cookie banner, or a terms or privacy page.
  • It finds the documents the box links to, reads them, and lists what matters: for example arbitration, selling your data, or a broad licence over what you post.
  • Each point is one plain sentence with the exact words from the document, so you can check it yourself.
  • If it finds nothing, it says that does not mean there is no risk.

Private by design

  • Everything is worked out on your device with fixed rules. No AI service, no account, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Nothing is saved unless you turn that on.
  • Details are in the privacy policy.
Not a lawyer. Term Sherpa is a plain-English guide, not a lawyer. It isn’t legal advice and can miss or misread things. Read the full terms, and ask a lawyer about anything important.

Term Sherpa

Welcome to Term Sherpa

Term Sherpa explains the terms of service, privacy policy or "I agree" box you are looking at, in plain English.

  • It only looks when you click its icon.
  • Nothing leaves your device. No accounts, no tracking, nothing about what you read is sent to anyone.
  • Nothing is saved by default. When you close this window, it is forgotten.
  • The only time it goes online is to open a terms page you ask it to read, and your browser asks you first.
